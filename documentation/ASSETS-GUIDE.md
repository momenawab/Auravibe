# Assets Guide for Aura Vibe

## 📦 What Assets You Need

### 1. **Logo** (Priority: HIGH)
**Location:** `assets/images/`
- **logo.png** or **logo.svg** - Main logo (transparent background)
- **Size:** 200x80px or similar ratio
- **Format:** PNG (with transparency) or SVG (best)
- **You already have:** Your gold "AV" logo with watch

### 2. **Watch Product Images** (Priority: HIGH)
**Location:** `uploads/products/`
- At least 8-12 high-quality watch photos
- **Size:** 800x800px minimum (square format)
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Quality:** High resolution, white/clean background
- **Examples needed:**
  - Luxury gold chronograph
  - Diver watch
  - Classic dress watch
  - Sport watch
  - Limited edition pieces

### 3. **Category Images** (Priority: MEDIUM)
**Location:** `assets/images/`
- `category-luxury.jpg` - Luxury watches category
- `category-sport.jpg` - Sport watches category
- `category-classic.jpg` - Classic watches category
- `category-limited.jpg` - Limited edition category
- **Size:** 600x800px (portrait) or 800x600px (landscape)
- **Format:** JPG

### 4. **3D Watch Models** (Priority: LOW - Optional)
**Location:** `assets/models/`
- GLB or GLTF format
- For interactive 3D product viewer
- **Sources:**
  - Sketchfab.com (free models available)
  - CGTrader.com
  - TurboSquid.com
  - Or create custom in Blender

### 5. **Favicon** (Priority: LOW)
**Location:** `assets/images/`
- `favicon.png` or `favicon.ico`
- **Size:** 32x32px or 64x64px
- Small version of your logo

## 💡 Pro Tips

### For Best Results:
- **Consistency:** All product images should have similar lighting/background
- **Quality:** Use at least 800x800px images
- **Format:** JPG for photos, PNG for logos with transparency
- **Optimization:** Compress images before uploading (use TinyPNG.com)
